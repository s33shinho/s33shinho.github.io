1.7.1 | Nov 25, 2026

NewA new account starts on a welcome page that suggests importing from Steam, explains how the import works and that prices and dates stay yours to change.
NewImport from Steam is in the main menu, next to Add a game and in the account menu.
NewLibraries that were never compared with Steam get a one-time note about the import, which can be closed.

1.7.0 | Nov 25, 2026

NewImport from Steam: paste the link to your Steam profile and pick the games to add. Steam does not share purchase dates or prices, so imported games come in without them and you add both yourself.
NewThe import compares your Steam account with your library: games already there are left out, similar titles (such as a sequel) are shown for you to check, and Steam games in your library that are not on the account are listed.
NewLibrary check has a button that shows only games without a purchase date or price in the list.
ChangedGames without a price show No price and are left out of the average and median price, and games without a date say No date.
ChangedThe FAQ and the privacy policy describe Import from Steam.
