Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short
  • We keep your e-mail, nickname and game library so the app can sync them between your devices.
  • Nothing is sold. No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
  • The public ranking and the public link are off until you switch them on.
  • Google (Firebase) stores the data; Cloudflare and GitHub host the site.
  • You can export or delete everything yourself under Your account, or write to info@backlogwallet.com.

The full text follows.

1. Who is responsible

Backlog Wallet is a small, non-commercial project run by an individual developer, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at info@backlogwallet.com or through the Feedback & contact page.

2. What data we process

  • Account: your e-mail address, your password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it in plain text), your nickname, your library, savings goals, display settings (currency, theme), the date you registered, whether your e-mail is verified, and the time you accepted these terms. A record linking your nickname to your e-mail address is kept so that you can log in with your nickname and so that nicknames stay unique.
  • Public ranking (optional, off by default): only if you switch it on in your account, your nickname, the total value of your library in EUR and your number of games are shown to everyone on the Ranking page.
  • Public sharing (optional, off by default): only if you switch it on, anyone who has your link can see your nickname and game list (never your e-mail).
  • Feedback form: the subject and message you type (and the e-mail address, if you give one) are sent to us by e-mail through Web3Forms. Please do not include sensitive data.
  • Visit counter: we count how many sessions start per day. The counter is a single number per day and contains no personal data.
  • Server logs: our hosting providers (Cloudflare and GitHub) process technical request data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site. We do not have access to these logs for profiling.
  • What we do not do: we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, we do not build profiles, and we make no automated decisions about you. Since version 1.8.11 we no longer determine your country from your IP address; a value stored earlier is deleted automatically the next time you log in.

3. Cookies and local storage

The site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. It uses your browser's local storage (and, for the login session, the storage of the Firebase Authentication library) only for technical purposes you asked for or that are needed to run the app: theme, currency, your "remember me" choice, dismissed hints, menu state, a short anti-spam timer, the fact that you have seen the storage notice, your login session and, for the visit counter, a one-session flag. Because this storage is strictly necessary or requested by you, no consent banner is required; the notice only informs you. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings, or from the Privacy Policy inside the app: clear local data on this device (this removes everything this site has saved on this device and signs you out; your account data is not affected).

4. Services we use and who receives data

  • Google (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore): stores accounts and libraries.
  • Cloudflare: DNS and security proxy in front of the site. GitHub (GitHub Pages): hosts the site files.
  • Web3Forms: forwards feedback messages to our inbox.
  • Resources loaded by your browser: the charts library from jsDelivr, the Firebase code from Google (gstatic.com) and currency rates from open.er-api.com. When the page loads, these providers receive your IP address and basic browser information, as with any website resource.
  • Buy Me a Coffee and Ko-fi: only if you click a donation link; their own privacy policies then apply.

We do not sell your data and do not share it for marketing. Your data is not made public except through the optional ranking and sharing features above.

5. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): running your account and syncing your library.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): the public ranking and public sharing. You can withdraw consent at any time by switching the option off; the ranking entry is then deleted.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): keeping the site secure and available, preventing abuse, the aggregated visit counter, loading the third-party resources listed above, and answering your messages.

6. How long we keep data

Account and library data: until you delete your account. Ranking entry: until you switch it off or delete your account. Deleting your account from "Your account" removes your profile, library, ranking entry and nickname record; copies may remain in provider backups for a limited period under the provider's own rules. Feedback e-mails: up to 12 months after your last message, then they are deleted. Local browser data: until you clear it.

7. International transfers

Google, Cloudflare, GitHub and Web3Forms may process data outside the EEA/Switzerland, including in the United States. Where this happens we rely on the provider's safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and port your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Export and deletion are available to you directly in "Your account"; for anything else write to info@backlogwallet.com and we will answer within one month. You may complain to the data-protection supervisory authority in your country of residence (for example UODO in Poland, the FDPIC in Switzerland).

9. Age

The App is not intended for children. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with Firebase's security features and access rules; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, the date at the top is updated and significant changes are listed in the changelog.
