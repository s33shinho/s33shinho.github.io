Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. It is a hobby project. You need an account to use it, and the account is free.

Can I import my Steam purchases?

Sort of. Steam has no official export, but Smart import lets you paste your purchase history (or any order list) and it picks out titles, prices and dates. You review the result before anything is added.

I buy in different currencies. Does that work?

Yes. Each game keeps the price and currency you paid in. Totals are converted with live rates from open.er-api.com, and if that service is down the app falls back to saved rates.

Where is my data stored?

In Firebase (Google Cloud), behind access rules, so only your account can read your library. You can download everything as JSON at any time.

What happens if I delete my account?

Everything is removed. The app downloads a backup of your data first, so you can import it again later if you change your mind.

Can I switch between light and dark?

Yes, with the moon/sun button at the top. You can also set your nickname and preferred currency in your account.

Can I cheat the ranking?

Not easily, but it is not impossible either. Your entry is worked out from your own library in your browser, and obviously broken values are rejected, but a determined person could still fake one. It is a ranking for fun, and entries that look fake can be removed.
