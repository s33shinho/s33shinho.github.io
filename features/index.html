What Backlog Wallet does

A ledger for the games you buy. Log what you paid, where you bought it and when. Backlog Wallet adds it up, converts it to your currency and shows how much of your library is still unplayed. It is free.

Purchases

Log the title, price, currency, store, date and whether you own it digitally or physically. Add games one at a time, paste a whole list, or use Smart import on a Steam purchase history and review the result before anything is saved.

Statuses

Mark each game as completed, in progress, not completed, endless or one you will not play. Set statuses in bulk for many games at once.

Currencies

Prices stay exactly as you paid them. Totals can be shown in any of 20+ currencies using live exchange rates, with saved rates as a fallback when you are offline.

Spending charts

Spending per month, per year and per store, digital versus physical, and year-over-year comparison. Every chart can be saved as a PNG.

Cost insights

Cost per completed game, the value of your unplayed backlog, and how this year compares with the same period last year.

Year in Review

A summary of your year in games: total spent, number of purchases, favorite platform, most active month and priciest purchase, with a purchase heatmap and slides you can download as images.

Savings goals

Save up for a game you want and track how far along you are.

Virtual shelf, achievements and What to play?

See your library as a shelf of generated covers, unlock badges as it grows, or let the app pick something from your backlog when you cannot decide.

Sync and backup

Your library lives in your account, so it is the same on your phone and your computer. Changes made on two devices are merged. Export to JSON or CSV whenever you like.

Installable and works offline

Backlog Wallet can be installed like an app from your browser and opens without a network connection.

Private by design

Only your account can read your library. You can share a read-only public link if you choose to, take part in the optional ranking, and delete your account and all data at any time.
